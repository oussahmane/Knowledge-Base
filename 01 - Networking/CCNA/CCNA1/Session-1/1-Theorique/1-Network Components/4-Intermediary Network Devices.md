
- An intermediary device interconnects end devices. Example include switches, wireless access points, routers, and firewalls.

- Management of data as it flows through a network is also the role of an intermediary device, including:
	- Regenerate and retransmit data signals.
	- Maintain information about what pathways exist in the network.
	- Notify other devices of errors and communication failures.

- Intermediary Devices Example:
	- Wireless Router
	- Lan Switch
	- Router
	- Multilayer Switch
	- Firewall Appliance
